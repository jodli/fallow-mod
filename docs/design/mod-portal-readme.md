# Mod Portal README (draft)

Everything below the line is the portal text.

---

*A story mod for Factorio: Space Age.*

You wake up in the grass next to a crashed ship, with smoke in the air and no idea how you got there. In the cargo hold, strapped into a cradle that survived the fall, sits a sealed container. Nothing you own will open it. It is faintly warm, and it is the only warm thing in the wreck.

Deeper in the hull, behind a door you can force, a console is waiting for power. Give it some, and a voice starts talking.

> "Power. Three percent. Hull. No. Crew. No data. You. You: here. Good. One is a start."

The voice belongs to the ship. It has forgotten its own name and why you fell, and it has no idea what the container is for. Neither do you. You'll find out together, one repair and one world at a time.

## From the ship's log

> **What I need from you**
>
> **Repair me.** I'm spread across this wreck in more pieces than I can count, and I've tried. Every part you fix gives me back a machine, a sense or a memory. I'll tell you about the memories. Most of them.
>
> **Make copies.** The fabricator wants to copy the container. I can't read its instructions, but I can tell you what it asks for: living tissue from this world, grown close to your factory's smoke. The smoke will also bring the locals, so build walls.
>
> **Take the copies to the other worlds.** My ovens for heat, current, cold and growing were crushed in the fall. The planets still have all four. Vulcanus has the heat, Fulgora has the lightning, Gleba grows anything and Aquilo freezes it. The copies need each of those, in the right order, and they fade if you take too long.
>
> **Study them.** Something that is trying this hard to become the container has a lot to teach. Your labs will want the copies.
>
> **Take the container too.** I want to know what it does on other worlds. Run the experiment wherever I ask, and bring me what you find. I can't hear you out there, so you'll have to come home and tell me.
>
> **Look where I point.** Once I can see again, I'll mark the places I want you to visit.
>
> **Listen.** Out past the cold, something has been calling for a long time.

> **What you should know about me**
>
> I need Factorio: Space Age to run.
>
> My story starts at the crash, so a new game is the best way to meet me.
>
> If there are more of you, I'll talk to all of you. Anyone can bring me what they find, and whatever I remember goes into the log for everyone.
>
> Everything I tell you stays in my log, so nothing is lost if you're busy fighting when I talk. Sometimes I'll want to show you something. You can always look away, and it'll be waiting in the log.
>
> I'm still being repaired, and so is this mod. Expect missing pieces.

One day the ship will ask you for something. What you hand it is up to you.
